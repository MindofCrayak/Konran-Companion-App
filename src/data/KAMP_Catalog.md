## **Konran Academy of Might & Proficiency (K.A.M.P.)**

### **Academic Catalog, Core Curriculum & Credit-Based Adventuring Paths**

### **Overview: The 5-Term Credit-Based Framework**

Attendance at K.A.M.P. spans **5 full terms** (scholastic years). The curriculum merges a traditional CORE curriculum in adventuring training and preparation, but then also allows students the ability to grow their specialities in existing or emerging fields that include but are not limited to the studies of advanced sciences, arcane/divine/psionic arts in an addition to Hearth & Home Studies, Entertainment/Philanthropy and Civil Service.  

**The Standard Five Terms**                       

**Terms 1–2**  \| Mandatory Core Curriculum & Foundational Adventuring Readiness  
**Terms 2–3** \| Open Credit Electives: Beginner (B) & Advanced (A) Tracks  
**Terms 4–5** \| Specialized Capstones & Single Mastery (M) Intensive Study  
**Term 5 Final** \| Adventurer Guild Commission OR The Masquerade Open Auditions

#### **Academic Freedom & House Neutrality**

Unlike rigid institutions, K.A.M.P. utilizes an **Open Credit System**. While students reside in one of four residential Houses (**Gryffon**, **Sphynx**, **Hydra**, or **Unicorn**), **a student’s House does NOT limit their course enrollment**.  
A student in House Gryffon may spend their credits on Arcane Dueling, Philanthropy, or Bardic Performance without restriction. This credit system encourages **meta-multiclassing**, allowing students to tailor an adaptable hybrid transcript to fit their personal adventuring goals.

But there is always honor in those that choose to follow the strict “path” of their house. 

### **I. Core Curriculum (Mandatory Terms 1 & 2\)**

Every student entering K.A.M.P. must complete six mandatory core courses (12 total Core Credits). These establish basic survival capability, physical conditioning, magitech literacy, tactical critical thinking, and cultural awareness across Konran.

**MANDATORY CORE COURSES**                                    
**Course Code & Title \| Primary Focus**      
CORE-101: Survivalism  \| Biome Hazards & Foraging  
CORE-102: Hearth & Field Triage     \| Domestic Alchemy & Field Salves  
CORE-103: Life, Cultures & Ethics   \| Realm Sociology & Public Ethics  
CORE-104: History & Applied Tactics \| Conflict Analysis & Zyng\! Team  
CORE-105: Martial Practice & Arena  \| Physical Literacy & Squads       
CORE-106: Celestial Navigation 101  \| Ether Tracking & Astronomy    

* **CORE-101: Survivalism 101**  
  * **Instructors:** Aukymba (Mother), & Rothar Earthshaper  
  * **Overview:** Transitions into wilderness survival across Konran’s biomes—teaching students land navigation, weather reading, and harvesting raw ingredients in the Thyrst River forest.  
* **CORE-102: Hearth & Field Triage**  
  * **Instructors:** Chef Ignatius Flamestove, Nuria Althea, & Syster Servytude  
  * **Overview:** Domestic home economics meets field medicine. Students process raw flora, game, and minerals harvested in *Survivalism* into nutritional biome rations, coagulant bandages, basic restorative salves, and simple alchemical brews.  
* **CORE-103: Life, Cultures & Public Ethics**  
  * **Instructors:** Ambassador Elowen Starflower, Counselor Elynn Gloomshroud, Pryme Kama, & Lucia Dawnshroud  
  * **Overview:** Foundational social studies covering inter-species customs, diplomatic treaties, and religious rites across the Leste and Vian Provinces. Includes psychological wellness, emotional regulation, and civic responsibility.  
* **CORE-104: History & Applied Tactics**  
  * **Instructor:** Professor Yvctus  
  * **Overview:** Analysis of historic battles paired with modern conflict theory. Teaches critical thinking under stress, evaluating non-standard threat vulnerabilities (beasts, undead, automatons), and basic game theory for *Zyng\!*  
* **CORE-105: Martial Practice & Arena Athletics**  
  * **Instructors:** Commander Lysander Lyteheart & Coach Thrain Ironarm  
  * **Overview:** Physical conditioning, defensive maneuvers, and basic weapon handling in the Great Glass Arena. Emphasizes defensive formations, squad cohesion, and small-unit teamwork.  
* **CORE-106: Celestial Navigation 101**  
  * **Instructor:** Olloppa  
  * **Overview:** Conducted in the offshore Lighthouse optical labs. Teaches star-mapping, tracking celestial alignments, reading magitech compasses, and navigating without terrestrial landmarks.

While students are encouraged to experiment and find what suits them, the school also provides a basic Path of courses that follows the house they’re sorted into. These Paths becoming patterns set by years of previous students and their class selections.

### **II. Departmental Course Catalog**

Elective courses are categorized into   
**Beginner (B: 2 Credits)**, **Advanced (A: 3 Credits)**, and **Mastery (M: 5 Credits)** levels.

**Academic Regulation:** Because **Mastery (M)** courses require extensive lab hours, rare crystal consumption (such as Vertazyum or Kraykatzyte), and field expedition duty, students are strictly limited to **one Mastery course** during their senior terms (Terms 4–5).

### **III. Credit-Based Degree Requirements & Multiclassing Mechanics**

To graduate from K.A.M.P., a student must earn a minimum of **30 Academic Credits** over 5 terms.

#### **Credit Distribution Table**            

Requirement Category \-  Credits Needed            
Mandatory Core Curriculum \- 12 Credits                  
Open Electives (B & A Tiers) \- 13–15 Credits       
Senior Mastery Capstone (M) \- 5 Credits  
TOTAL FOR GRADUATION  \- 30+ Credits       

#### **How Multiclassing Works at K.A.M.P.**

Because House assignment does not restrict course enrollment, students can mix and match disciplines to create custom adventuring archetypes:

* **The Spellblade / Arcane Tactician:**  
  *Core (12)* \+ *GRY-202: Advanced Combat (3)* \+ *HYD-101: Magic Basics (2)* \+ *GRY-210: Zyng\! Practicum (3)* \+ *SPH-110: Tinkering (2)* \+ *GRY-300: Tactical Mastery (5)* \= **27+ Credits**  
* **The Corporate Paladin / Inquisitor:**  
  *Core (12)* \+ *DIV-101: Divine Basics (2)* \+ *LEG-210: Regional Law & Salvage (3)* \+ *GRY-202: Advanced Combat (3)* \+ *DIV-300G: Sacred Order (Good) (5)* \+ *LEG-300: Corporate Pacts (5)* \= **30 Credits**  
* **The Infiltrator Bard / Masquerade Agent:**  
  *Core (12)* \+ *UNI-101: Acoustic Theory (2)* \+ *UNI-210: Subterfuge (3)* \+ *UNI-202: Masquerade Pageantry (3)* \+ *LEG-110: Philanthropy (2)* \+ *UNI-300: Virtuoso & Shadowcraft (5)* \= **25+ Credits**

### **IV. Graduation Pathways**                                         

### **Two Pathways at Term 5 Graduation**

> 1. **The Adventuring Guild Commission:** The classic K.A.M.P. route\[cite: 1\]. Graduates receive certified Adventuring Crests, forming official field bands to accept high-tier guild contracts, explore unmapped wilderness, and handle regional security across Konran.  
> 2. **The Masquerade Open Audition Showcase:** For senior students seeking elite corporate or divine patronage, master apprenticeships, or entry into the sovereign floating city of Celeste. Students present an Open Expedition Showcase during the annual 1-day stop to secure sponsorships and titles (*Legend*, *Primadonna*, *Tactician Master*) within the traveling capital.
