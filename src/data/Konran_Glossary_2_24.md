## New Player Introduction

	*Hello,* the voice whispered from just behind your ear. The dark was almost enough to be scary, but it wasn’t. And the voice wasn’t a whisper, or a taunt. It was calm, loud but not harsh and friendly, almost intimate. 

*Welcome, and congratulations\!*  
*You’ve been selected for an amazing journey, a journey of Fate.* 

*But you think, my family\! My loved ones\! They depend on me\!*   
*True, they did. But they do not any longer. It is fate that you are here. Not choice. Your destiny is meant to be elsewhere, to be more. To be greater than you understand. If to be a direct role, or a more passive building of the foundation you have been chosen by the great Nine Gods to come and live on the capital, to come to the plane of Konran.* 

*Here on the Capital, there are many wonders in development, but this plane is not like yours. There are no ancient ruins. Not much history. This world was built to house and home all of the gods creation in a safe place. A Place of dreams, that’s what the capital stands for, the best of the best. Something of you, your seed, your mind, your hands, your gifts. Something will help make Konran great, if not today, tomorrow. If not tomorrow, a hundred years from now, your descendants will rule, will matter.* 

*But with a fresh foundation lies dangers. This world is savage. Outside the safety of the cities, outside the gaze of the seeding races is nothing but flora and fauna. Be warned, dark forest harbor dark silhouettes.  New cities are being founded everyday through the help of The Churches, and more importantly communities. Some settlements even though very young already appear as if here for decades if not longer. Races of all kinds, followers of all Gods, a world built on unity and trust.* 

*The only true law of Konran stands at do not murder for sport or pleasure, of thinking race, or beast.* 

*While you are no longer bound by the deity of your native plane, you are welcome to involve all the Gods, or declare yourself for one of the churches directly. Here on Konran sit places of advancement for the study in arts held high by the divine.*   
*Declaring gives you access to these arts and teachers, a welcoming hall and bed at most cities and more.*   
*Not being Declared allows you a welcomed place at most churches, and good tidings as well as freedom to invoke the names of the gods when in times of need for their blessing.* 

*No job is too great here on the capital, for no creature is too small of a destiny to be here.* 

*Welcome, to your future, your destiny and your fate\!* 

Suddenly there is a soft shudder and the darkness starts to move around you and focus. A thin white crack breaks slowly and the light floods your eyes blinding you for a moment. 

You blink and squint, the shimmering bluish pearl white of the sun fades on the royal blue sky. In close orbit looms a large white moon. Greater out barely visible on the horizon is the corner of a smaller part of purple in the sky, hinting another moon. The grass, green and blue, thick and lush. The air fresh. The Road purple, stone but soft to walk on. It leads you, out into the frontier and to the doors of a nicely sized two story bar. The Sign reads “The Mydnight Rendezvous”.

You go inside, and a handsome blonde man with shimmering skin and bronze colored eyes smiles at you and welcomes to take a seat at the bar, he’s been waiting for you. 

## The Planes

### History

	The planes were forged and made in the images of their respective Gods. It is said that after the Union of the Nine and the pantheon was newly forged the Gods decided to travel through the Voyd. Krayvuk had the knowledge of how to escape and navigate the Voyd, but lacked the means. Ousava pulled the others close to her, one for each of her tentacles and pulled them close to her till they all were smashed together. With the magics of them all her ink sealed a cocoon that allowed them to travel the Voyd. 

When they decided to stop they found themselves in a system devoid of life, nine worlds. They started harvesting the planes to their core recourses, and harvest others in the near area as well for more matter as needed.

### Ayan

	The Sun of the system was strong, and young. Ousava first extinguished this light with the help of Ay. Their joining allowed it’s energy to not be lost to the planes around it or the space it occupied. With their focus the expanded and hammered a world into the core of this flame. A world inhabited by those entwined with Fate. The fires themselves have been rumored to not be fires but gateways to a dimension of evil and despair.   
   
Ay keeps the secrets of Ayan close to her saying that none more than necessary should know the plans of fate. 

### Kay-Kee

When Ousava turned her gaze to the plane closest to the sun it was noticed by Kayazoku. And in his excitement he leapt and grabbed the world at the same time as she. Being so hot and with their great strength without trying the world was torn in two. Before Ousava could fix it Kay let out a great tear and it fell onto one of the halves drowning it in water. “NOW I HAVE TWO” he cried, and wouldn’t allow Ousava to mend them.

#### Kay

Kay is the Top portion of this strange world. Hot and dry this desert world is shaped like a mushroom and most of its metropolises are hidden deep within the earth. Thri kreen, Githyanki and other insect races and warlord battle for control of the great hives. All while scavengers, Kitsune and other races find their homes on or near the surface. The world is managed and ruled by only chaos and total disorder. No laws or true rules apply. Communities and towns make their own laws, but they can always be challenged as there are no rules. 

#### Kee

Kee is the Bottom portion of this strange world. Wet and dreary this world is ruled with an iron fist of a social hierarchy. The Kits, an acnet race cousins of elves at one point have evolve to the point of mental domination and superiority but lack physicality. Through there will they enslaved the massive Kitsune population and uses them as a shadowy police force leaving this water world and it’s islands under Kit rule only. Strict order and law rule this world

##### The Passing

Once every year, Kay and Kee run perfectly aligned with each other for a whole day. Allowing those on the Mushroom like Kay and the Bowl like Kee to jump worlds. Leaving Order for Chaos or vice versa hoping that life on the other is better than what they’re used to. 

### Synthelavor

Synthelavor is the plane Slavyna rules with an iron hoof. Second plane to round the fire of Ay, the plane of Synthelavor is at it’s core home to Ousava’s elemental plane of fire. This leaves Synthelavor with a rather toxic and hot atmosphere, skyline lined with mountains, mines and skyscrapers. Every resource of the plane is under constant harvest by the demons and orcs that call this plane their native home. 

Small villages and cities are never far from every major work site. Typically populated by pregnant prostitutes and bastards. 

Five moons circle Synthelavor in between the glittering red dust rings that spiral around the fiery world. 

#### Tatyon

This moon circles close and houses the finest schools and finishing academies ever known, here you can also find the Lust Wraiths. 

#### Odahs

This dark rock in fact is a moon of Kark and hardly sees light making it a choice position for the School of Shadows. Not much is known of this place. 

#### Kark

This larger moon is quite comfortable with lakes and natural hot springs and is treated as a training and leisure location for The Dark Legion

#### Syndavaria

Slavyna’s personal Capital world, bathed in beauty and wealth. 

#### Hemalod

The farthest moon is mainly oceans and lakes and known to be home to the Brotherhood of Blood. A small moon populated with shadow elves, orcs, tieflings, and other sentient creatures. Temperate and typically used for breeding purposes. 

### Lona Uyn

Lona Uyn a terrestrial world reminiscent of most blue waters and green landed planes. While beautiful the surface is craggy and flat with random outcroppings of rocks. Natural fissures of water appeared to glow brighter when indigenous life came to drink from them.   
Bioluminescent crystal lattices peppered the expanse humming with energy. A vast network of tunnels and cities exist beneath the surface. The more notable sight however was the aerial rock formations floating freely above surface. Thriving cities of many different races flourish on each. Three moons orbit this planet.

#### Kelth

This moon is made of gold and of course is the location of the finest mint and treasury in all the lands. An indigenous race known as the Hayde call this their home. They are a race of genies that oversee the production and conservation of wealth. 

#### Zorm

Planet of the Forges. All who consider themselves the creators and inventors of the future find themselves drawn here. A rocky desolate moon transformed into endless workshops even underground.

#### Sylef

The furthest to the planet. home of the Wise Elves who are noble, extremely smart and ruled by the night.  They have for the most part kept to themselves. Only recently have they been noticed taking positions of high rank out in the world. 

### Konran

You are here\!   
Konran is the world that was built last. Using the core of the original 4th plane this world was chosen to be the Capital of the Pantheon. Ousava took her time with its construction and of course negotiated all the demands of the others to be built into one working world. A world of freedom and power. Konran represents the dreams of the Gods. 

Konran has two moons. Both have remained unnamed by the people of the world. 

#### Luna Prime

A small white moon, it quickly passes over the surface timing two full rotations per standard Konran day. In a diagonal from north to south and then south to north. Said to be the location and base of the mysterious Dyvine Council. 

#### Luna Beta

A large purple gas giant, not much has been known or observed besides topical storms. 

### Vaya Mar

The Aquatic world of Vaya Mar caters mostly to the full aquatic races. It has two seasons, a wet season and a dry season. A majority of its population living underwater it’s surface is dotted with massive floating shipwreck cities.

When wet, storms and massive hurricanes are in constant motion circling the nearly flooded surface with rain and wind. The storms are actively tracked in real time by most of the population, although most can and do steer clear of the storms, there are those who take their chances. While at the height of wet season, only 6% of the surface has land visible, typically being only peaks and tips of mountains, nothing as large as an acre.

When the dry season starts, the massive main moon Lantyca circles with dangerous proximity to the water giant. So close that it’s speed and mass pull the tides up through the sky themselves. The hurricanes clear and massive waterspouts open up filling the skies, blocking the sun for a fleeting few hours, long enough for trillions of gallons to transfer from one plane to another. Once past its apex the moon breaks gravity and pulls the tides. Revealed plateaus, mountains, valleys and islands that can now be used and scavenged by the amphibious races. Once these islands and dry land appears a very competitive scavenging season starts where countless lost fortunes can be recovered under the laws of ocean salvage. 

Wet season and dry season each last 8 months.

#### Lantyca

A massive and dense world of plant life and rainforest, Lantyca requires constant supplies of water.

Mount Kraykaty's story begins as a formidable volcano, undergoing the natural cycles of its fiery existence. However, during the first age, a cataclysmic event forever altered its destiny. Found exclusively within the Kray Province, this extraordinary mineral has become an object of fascination and curiosity for scholars and alchemists alike.

On the other hand, Pyzrate, a captivating purple crystal, holds immense power but carries a dangerous caveat. When brought into contact with Kraykatzyte, Pyzrate unleashes catastrophic effects, generating massive and opposing gravitational forces that result in violent repulsion.
