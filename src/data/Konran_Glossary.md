# The Konran Saga: Tales of the Fated

## New Player Introduction

	*Hello,* the voice whispered from just behind your ear. The dark was almost enough to be scary, but it wasn’t. And the voice wasn’t a whisper, or a taunt. It was calm, loud but not harsh and friendly, almost intimate. 

*Welcome, and congratulations\!*  
*You’ve been selected for an amazing journey, a journey of Fate.* 

*But you think, my family\! My loved ones\! They depend on me\!*   
*True, they did. But they do not any longer. It is fate that you are here. Not choice. Your destiny is meant to be elsewhere, to be more. To be greater than you understand. If to be a direct role, or a more passive building of the foundation you have been chosen by the great Nine Gods to come and live on the capital, to come to the plane of Konran.* 

*Here on the Capital, there are many wonders in development, but this plane is not like yours. There are no ancient ruins. Not much history. This world was built to house and home all of the gods creation in a safe place. A Place of dreams, that’s what the capital stands for, the best of the best. Something of you, your seed, your mind, your hands, your gifts. Something will help make Konran great, if not today, tomorrow. If not tomorrow, a hundred years from now, your descendants will rule, will matter.* 

*But with a fresh foundation lies dangers. This world is savage. Outside the safety of the cities, outside the gaze of the seeding races is nothing but flora and fauna. Be warned, dark forest harbor dark silhouettes.  New cities are being founded everyday through the help of The Churches, and more importantly communities. Some settlements even though very young already appear as if here for decades if not longer. Races of all kinds, followers of all Gods, a world built on unity and trust.* 

*The only true law of Konran stands at do not murder for sport or pleasure, of thinking race, or beast.* 

*While you are no longer bound by the deity of your native plane, you are welcome to involve all the Gods, or declare yourself for one of the churches directly. Here on Konran sit places of advancement for the study in arts held high by the divine.*   
*Declaring gives you access to these arts and teachers, a welcoming hall and bed at most cities and more.*   
*Not being Declared allows you a welcomed place at most churches, and good tidings as well as freedom to invoke the names of the gods when in times of need for their blessing.* 

*No job is too great here on the capital, for no creature is too small of a destiny to be here.* 

*Welcome, to your future, your destiny and your fate\!* 

Suddenly there is a soft shudder and the darkness starts to move around you and focus. A thin white crack breaks slowly and the light floods your eyes blinding you for a moment. 

You blink and squint, the shimmering bluish pearl white of the sun fades on the royal blue sky. In close orbit looms a large white moon. Greater out barely visible on the horizon is the corner of a smaller part of purple in the sky, hinting another moon. The grass, green and blue, thick and lush. The air fresh. The Road purple, stone but soft to walk on. It leads you, out into the frontier and to the doors of a nicely sized two story bar. The Sign reads “The Mydnight Rendezvous”.

You go inside, and a handsome blonde man with shimmering skin and bronze colored eyes smiles at you and welcomes to take a seat at the bar, he’s been waiting for you. 

## The Planes

### History

	The planes were forged and made in the images of their respective Gods. It is said that after the Union of the Nine and the pantheon was newly forged the Gods decided to travel through the Voyd. Krayvuk had the knowledge of how to escape and navigate the Voyd, but lacked the means. Ousava pulled the others close to her, one for each of her tentacles and pulled them close to her till they all were smashed together. With the magics of them all her ink sealed a cocoon that allowed them to travel the Voyd. 

When they decided to stop they found themselves in a system devoid of life, nine worlds. They started harvesting the planes to their core recourses, and harvest others in the near area as well for more matter as needed.

### Ayan

	The Sun of the system was strong, and young. Ousava first thought to extinguish this light with the help of Ay, but found that they both favored repurposing the energy as opposed to destroying it. Their joining allowed it’s energy to not be lost to the planes around, and channeled it or the space it occupied. With their focus they expanded and wove the fires around a world into the core of the flame. A world inhabited by those entwined with Fate. The fires themselves have been rumored to not be fires but gateways to a dimension of evil and despair. Some say light travels in great highways and tubes that allow travelers on the surface to move at speeds unheard of in the other realms.  
   
Ay keeps the secrets of Ayan close to her saying that none more than necessary should know the plans of fate. 

The sun burns bright white and thin wisp of blue and yellow. It’s rays and carefully woven, light being used more like threads than rays. Stitches and weaves of the surface mimic storms and the boiling seas of heat yo’d find in organic suns.  

### Kay-Kee

When Ousava turned her gaze to the plane closest to the sun it was noticed by Kayazoku. And in his excitement he leapt and grabbed the world at the same time as she. Being so hot and with their great strength without trying their world was torn in two. #### Kay

Kay is the Top portion of this strange world. Hot and dry this desert world is shaped like a mushroom and most of its metropolises are hidden deep within the earth. Thri kreen, Githyanki and other insect races and warlord battle for control of the great hives. All while scavengers, Kitsune and other races find their homes on or near the surface. The world is managed and ruled by only chaos and total disorder. No laws or true rules apply. Communities and towns make their own laws, but they can always be challenged as there are no rules. 

#### Kee

Kee is the Bottom portion of this strange world. Wet and dreary this world is ruled with an iron fist of a social hierarchy. The Kits, an acnet race cousins of elves at one point have evolved to the point of mental domination and superiority but lack physicality. Through there will they enslaved the massive Kitsune population and uses them as a shadowy police force leaving this water world and it’s islands under Kit rule only. Strict order and law rule this world

Kee appears a stone basin over flowing. Masterful built cities lurk uder the weaters of the seas, while the marsh swamp lands of the surface are dotted with many oceans and seas. Fleets of ships and floating cities roam doing trade. Storms are prevalentand frequenst lasting most the year, becoming more frequent and terrible till the passing. 

##### The Passing

Once every year, Kay and Kee run perfectly aligned with each other for a whole week. Allowing those on the Mushroom like Kay and the Bowl like Kee to jump worlds. Leaving Order for Chaos or vice versa hoping that life on the other is better than what they’re used to.

You are here\!

#### Luna Beta

### Lantyca

The Aquatic world of Lantyca caters mostly to the full aquatic races. It has two seasons, a wet season and a dry season. A majority of its population living underwater it’s surface is dotted with massive floating shipwreck cities. But is mainly water and storms. 

When wet, storms and massive hurricanes are in constant motion circling the nearly flooded surface with rain and wind. The storms are actively tracked in real time by most of the population, although most can and do steer clear of the storms, there are those who take their chances. While at the height of wet season, only 6% of the surface has land visible, typically being only peaks and tips of mountains, nothing as large as an acre.

When dry season start, the massive main moon Vaya Mar circles with dangerous proximity to the water giant. So close that it’s speed and mass pull the tides up through the sky themselves. The hurricanes clear and massive waterspouts open up filling the skies, blocking the sun for a fleeting few hours, long enough for trillions of gallons to transfer from one plane to another. Once past its apex the moon breaks gravity and pulls the tides. Revealed plateaus, mountains, valleys and islands that can now be used and scavenged by the amphibious races. Once these islands and dry land appears a very competitive scavenging season starts where countless lost fortunes can recovered under the laws of ocean salvage. 

Wet season and dry season each last 8 months.

#### Vaya Mar

A massive and dense world of plant life and rainforest, Vaya Mar requires constant supplies of water which it gains every eight months with the passing of it's main world Lantyca. Vaya Mars small orbit meticulously calculated to support the refilling of the seas and shallow bays that dry and require filling. The tree cities are fitted with ballast containers rising with the rains as they fall loosely harnessed around the trunks of the massive trees. During the wet season there are blue seas and rivers that run over the typically just green landscape and horizon.

Kray Providence is an expansive continent situated entirely within the Western Hemisphere of this fantastical world, primarily located within the Southern Hemisphere, with a modest portion extending into the Northern Hemisphere. It is twice the size of an average continent and features a diverse array of geographical features and climates.

Kray Providence is encircled on the west by an extensive ocean and to the north and east by another vast body of water; the Northern Continent and a sizable sea lie to the northwest. The continent comprises numerous unique territories, each with its distinct culture, languages, and history.

Composed of two halves, on the west side and north, connected to the land canals of the Rycia Providence. The lush western half being a super rainforest and verdant forest of green foliage. But the land is unstable due to a magnetic compound found in the ground that causes gravity distortion. Skylands, plateaus and other levitation. Populated with many species, of note carnivorous plants, large super predator lizards/reptiles. At the center Mount KrayKaty.

Kray Providence spans an estimated 35,680,000 square kilometers (13,780,000 sq mi). Its population, as per the most recent theoretical estimate, is well above 868 million between all living and living dead/sentien spirits. Kray Providence is one of the largest continents in both area and population. The most populous territory carries nearly half of the continent's population and significantly contributes to the continent's GDP.

Most of Kray Providence's inhabitants live near the continent's western or eastern coasts, with the interior dominated by impressive natural barriers, such as a massive dormant super volcano and an enormous river system and towering skylands and plateaus. Western Kray Providence is known for its dense, sprawling rainforest, while the eastern half is defined by a mineral-rich desert. Most of the continent lies within the tropics, with the extremities in the north and south reaching into the temperate zones.

The cultural, ethnic, and social dynamics of Kray Providence are a result of interactions between native peoples, settlers, and immigrants, each contributing their unique traditions and influences. A handful of languages predominate due to the rich tapestry of its history. Despite geographical obstacles and cultural differences, Kray Providence has maintained relative peace in recent history, with few conflicts dotting its timeline.
