# **Academic Framework and Curriculum Catalogue of the Konran Academy of Might & Proficiency**

## **Institutional Architecture and Pedagogical Philosophy**

The Konran Academy of Might & Proficiency (K.A.M.P.) operates on a specialized credit-based academic matrix designed to unify scholastic theory with expeditionary field application1. The academy organizes its student body across four historic Residential Houses—Gryffon, Sphynx, Hydra, and Unicorn—which interface with five specialized degree-granting Colleges and autonomous administrative divisions1. While residential placement establishes a student's foundational peer community and baseline discipline, the Open Credit System guarantees that House affiliation never restricts enrollment across departmental lines1.

The academic model is engineered to support two distinct trajectories of character growth1. For scholars pursuing a pure House Path, the curriculum provides a concentrated single-class education1. By remaining dedicated to their parent college through graduation, these students end their scholastic journey between character levels 5 and 10 with deep class progression, including extra attacks, specialized subclass features, and established professional backgrounds1. Conversely, for students seeking hybrid proficiencies, spell-like abilities, or cross-disciplinary multiclassing, K.A.M.P.'s elective structure provides the narrative origin and mechanical justification for their emerging capabilities1. A martial combatant enrolling in foundational spell-weaving establishes the scholastic background for an Eldritch Knight or Bladesinger, while an arcane scholar studying covert reconnaissance builds the mechanical basis for an Arcane Trickster or Inquisitive operative1.

### **Five-Term Progression and Credit Architecture**

Graduation requires a minimum accumulation of 30 academic credits across a structured five-term sequence, corresponding directly to the physical dormitory levels of the campus1. During Years 1 and 2 (Terms 1 and 2), students complete 12 mandatory Core credits encompassing universal combat drills, field survivalism, emergency triage, institutional ethics, and celestial navigation1. This shared curriculum ensures that every matriculated student maintains baseline survival competency regardless of their eventual specialization1.  
At the start of Year 3 (Term 3), students formally declare their majors, transitioning into an open elective model where they schedule their own coursework across departmental boundaries1. Terms 3 and 4 (Years 3 and 4\) are dedicated to accumulating 13 to 15 credits of specialized Advanced (A) coursework and interdisciplinary electives1. In Term 5 (Year 5), students reach the capstone phase, where institutional policy strictly restricts each senior to a single Mastery (M) course worth 5 credits1. This limitation exists because Mastery tracks consume extensive campus resources, including rare crystalline materials and dedicated laboratory space, requiring intensive individual field practicums1.

### **Campus Lore, Institutional Safety, and Global Integration**

High-risk arcane, psionic, and martial instruction is made viable through institutional wards and campus safety protocols1. Upon matriculation, every student participates in the Covenant ritual, signing the academic register in blood2. This binding enchantment links the student body into a collective academic collective, dampening lethal kinetic surges, preventing accidental spell mortality, and legally enforcing the fundamental Capital Law of Konran: *"Do not murder for sport or pleasure"*1.  
Academic resources are managed through the KAMP Kodex, a crystalline data slate that serves as an interactive syllabus, library interface, and personal research ledger2. The institution maintains deep ties with external planar powers, recognizing the Nine Emissaries—including Celeste, Slavyna, Lyricia, Ousava, Psyon, Krayvuk, Oray, Kayzoku, and Ay—as the theological foundation of planetary law and divine energy channeling1.  
Each year, the traveling sovereign capital city of Celeste, known as The Masquerade, docks above the academy's Black Tower1. Graduating fifth-term seniors have the opportunity to present their capstone projects during the Masquerade Open Auditions, competing for elite corporate sponsorships, noble patronage, and planetary commissions, while other graduates receive direct Adventuring Guild Commissions for frontier deployment1.

## **Athletic Infrastructure: Curricular Combat Gym Versus Extracurricular Zyng\!**

The academy maintains an absolute curricular division between mandatory martial physical education ("Gym") and the competitive tactical sport of **Zyng\!**1.  
Curricular physical education classes within the College of Vanguard Tactics & Martial Prowess (GRY) focus strictly on lethal and non-lethal warfare mechanics, group combat cohesion, weapon mastery, shield-wall tactics, and heavy cavalry maneuvers1. These graded, credit-bearing courses (such as GRY-101 and GRY-220) are designed to prepare students for real-world planetary defense, dungeon exploration, and hostile border security1.  
In contrast, Zyng\! is an organized tactical athletic esport blending high-speed gymnastics, aerial kinetics, and live dungeoneering2. Matches occur within the Grand Arena, which features dynamic Silt-Silk hexagonal flooring capable of rising into 3D geometric terrain, anchored by a vertical kinetic rail known as the Silver Rail2. Teams, known as Companies, consist of nine players divided into two synchronized tactical units:

* **The Arena Team (5 Players):** Operates on the dynamic upper hex-floor, comprising one Bastion (heavy zone defender), two Slingers (high-mobility rail scorers), and two Shapers (terrain manipulators who reshape the hex-grid using resonant anklets)2.  
* **The Dungeon Party (4 Players):** Operates simultaneously in the subterranean gauntlet beneath the arena floor, comprising one Vanguard (frontline tank), one Cipher (scout and trap specialist), one Aegis (healer and defensive buffer), and one Conduit (arcane battery)2.

The objective of a Zyng\! match is to reach 1,000 points by maneuvering a charged kinetic energy ring (the Zyng) along the Silver Rail while the subterranean party clears dungeon traps, solves dynamic runes, and pacifies arena monsters to unlock scoring multipliers and power gates for their arena teammates2.  
Because Zyng\! is an athletic pursuit rather than core combat training, varsity practice and intramural matches are strictly extracurricular1. All athletic practices and scrimmages are scheduled during allocated afternoon free blocks (14:00 to 18:30)1.  
To participate in varsity athletics, intramural arena tournaments, or dungeon handling, students must complete the non-credit prerequisite course **Zyng 101 (EXT-101)**, which provides mandatory instruction on wingsuit flight mechanics, magnetic anchor gear, and arena safety codes1.

## **Strategic Recommendations for Curriculum Implementation**

To run an academic campaign within the K.A.M.P. setting, Game Masters should utilize the following structural procedures:

> 1. **Enforce Core Completion During Years 1 and 2:** Require all player characters to account for their 12 mandatory Core credits during character generation before unlocking Advanced (A) or Mastery (M) coursework1. This establishes consistent baseline competence across survival, field triage, and institutional ethics1.  
> 2. **Enforce Extracurricular Scheduling Boundaries:** Maintain the operational separation between combat "Gym" courses and Zyng\! athletic practices1. Enrolling in EXT-210: Zyng\! Varsity Practicum occupies the student’s afternoon free blocks (14:00 to 18:30), creating narrative trade-offs regarding when they can conduct covert library research, forge items, or undertake faction quests for faculty mentors1.  
> 3. **Restrict Mastery Tracks to Senior Capstones:** Uphold the institutional cap of **one Mastery (M) course** per student in Term 51. Mastery courses demand extensive material resources (such as Vertazyum or Kraykatzyte crystal matrices) and represent the student's terminal thesis1.  
> 4. **Tie In-Game Advancement to Academic Progression:** Replace arbitrary milestone level-ups with formal term examinations, patent approvals from Syster Satysfaction, and Masquerade audition rankings, directly linking character sheet progression to scholastic achievement within Konran Academy1.
