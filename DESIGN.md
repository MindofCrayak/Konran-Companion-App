# Design Document: ObsydianHeart Slablet MK IV & Konran OS

## 1. Architectural Overview
The ObsydianHeart Slablet MK IV is an in-world crystalpunk datapad and spatial desktop operating system. It provides a CRT phosphor-green and amber magitech interface for navigating character covenant trials, spatial windows, and astral horology.

## 2. Core Visual & Spatial Design System
- **Color Palette:** Phosphor Green (`#39ff14`), Arcane Amber (`#ffcc00`), Void Obsidian (`#0b0f19`), and Magitech Cyan (`#00e5a3`).
- **Typography:** Custom gothic/template serif headings paired with clean monospace terminal telemetry.
- **Spatial Canvas:** Persistent Three.js holographic globe of Planet Konran with synchronized orbiting moons (Olysphere and Ayanath) and interactive parallax.

## 3. Complications & Horology (Grand Chronometron)
- **36-Hour Diurnal Cycle:** 18-hour master chapter ring ("XIIX" at apex to "IX" at base) tracking Dawnward and Duskward solar passes.
- **Calendar & Pantheon:** 9 months of 27 days each, governed by The Nyne deities and 9 liturgical days.
- **Eccentric Regulators:** Concentric sub-dials with planetary wheels, celestial volvelles, and lunar orbit reticles.

## 4. Character & Academic Intake
- **Divine Covenant Registry:** The Fated Deity Quiz matching travelers to The Nyne.
- **K.A.M.P. Academy:** Academic intake spanning Hydra, Gryffon, Spynx, and Unycorn houses.

## 5. Spatial Canvas & Floating Resizable Nodes
- **Central Core:** Interactive Three.js holographic globe of Planet Konran with day/night atmospheric shaders and synchronized orbiting moons (Olysphere & Ayanath).
- **Blank Space & Globe Access:** Clicking the central planet or any vacant space on the canvas reveals the Terminal Hub context menu, allowing users to spawn, configure, and customize display screens.
- **Multi-Window Display Nodes:**
  * Free floating, draggable via grab headers, and smoothly resizable via edge/corner handles.
  * Node management: minimize to compact status pills, maximize to full canvas stage, cascade diagonally, or close.
  * Customization: each terminal node supports custom titles, color accents (Phosphor Green, Arcane Amber, Magitech Cyan, Celestial Violet, Solar Crimson), command console execution, and personal telemetry observation logs.
  * Native terminal applications: Fated Character Badge, Pantheon Codex, Grand Chronometron (36h horologium), K.A.M.P. Terminal, Obsydian Market Exchange, Adventuring Party Roster, and Faction Standings Ledger.
- **Menu Ribbons:** Persistent top status ribbon (system telemetry, active character pill, active node dock, mini-chronometron) and bottom menu ribbon (coordinates, moon indicators, quick ledger & node launch buttons).
