# Privacy Policy **Last Updated:** November 2026 The Konran Companion App ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App and how it is handled. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal user data. ### 1. Information We Collect and Process When you use the App, particularly when authenticating through Discord OAuth2, we may process: - **Discord User Profile:** Your public Discord User ID, username, display name, and avatar hash. - **Guild & Role Information:** Your server membership and assigned roles within the designated campaign server(s) to verify campaign access tiers. - **Player Ledger & Character Data:** Character names, level progression, and stats that you explicitly choose to store, synchronize with campaign ledgers, or export. - **Technical Logs:** Standard temporary server/edge operational logs (such as request timestamps and HTTP status codes) maintained automatically by infrastructure providers (Cloudflare Pages) for stability and debugging. ### 2. How Your Information Is Used We collect only what is strictly necessary to run the companion utilities: - To verify your role and campaign credentials. - To display your character, campaign notes, and party presence in the companion desktop interface. - To sync player state with the campaign game master ledger. ### 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing entities. Data is only communicated with the external services required for the app to function: - **Discord API:** To handle OAuth2 authentication and verify guild role permissions. - **LegendKeeper API:** To query campaign world lore and document entries. - **Cloudflare Pages / Google Sheets:** To route edge traffic and persist the campaign player ledger. ### 4. Data Retention & Deletion - Session tokens and local character edits are stored primarily in your browser's local memory / storage. - You may revoke the App's access at any time through your Discord account settings under **Authorized Apps**. - If you want your character record removed from the campaign ledger, contact the campaign administrator or game master. ### 5. Children's Privacy The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age requirement for Discord in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. ### 6. Updates to This Policy We may update this policy occasionally as new features are added. Continued use of the App following changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. ### 7. Contact For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, reach out via the Konran campaign Discord server.
